Keyboard shortcuts

SpaceStart recording this slide / stop recording. Starting also starts the teleprompter and the word pacer.
EnterKeep the take — uploads it and moves to the next slide.
DDiscard the take and stay here (redo). The discarded take is held in memory until you record again or leave the slide.
URestore the take you just discarded.
LListen back to the current take (press again to stop).
PRun the teleprompter without recording (practice), or pause it.
0Reset the teleprompter and word pacer to the top of this slide.
VSwitch between the scrolling teleprompter and the three-word pacer. Only one is ever on screen; the other stays paused in the background on the same clock, so switching mid-take does not change your position. Remembered across slides and reloads.
← →Previous / next slide. [ and ] do the same.
− +Slow down / speed up the prompter by 5 wpm (live, mid-take).
FFullscreen.
RToggle the readable script pane (full prose of this slide's script, word count, and estimated duration at 175 wpm).
SSwitch to the Player, same tab, on the same deck and slide. Blocked while recording; asks first if you have an unkept take.
? EscShow / hide this help.

Takes are written to _lectures/audio/<deck>/recorded/slide-NN.webm. The newest take always keeps that name; any previous take is renamed slide-NN-take2.webm, -take3, and so on. Nothing is ever overwritten or deleted.

Watch the level meter while you speak. If it stays flat, the microphone is not working — stop and fix it before recording the rest of the deck.

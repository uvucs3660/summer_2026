## Course philosophy

This is *Advanced* Web Development. The goal isn't to learn syntax — it's to build production-grade web systems and to speak the vocabulary of the industry that builds them.

Two pillars distinguish this version of the class:

1. **Vernacular as foundation.** In an LLM-mediated industry, fluency in the vocabulary of software engineering is the prerequisite for productive collaboration with AI. You will hear, write, and use the vocabulary of: the Agile Manifesto v2, Gang-of-Four design patterns, Enterprise Integration Patterns, the Perfect Framework, and Claude Code's own capability vocabulary in every part of the course.
2. **Claude Pro is your textbook.** No paid book. Maintain a Claude Pro subscription throughout the term and learn to leverage Claude Code as your primary development environment. You'll prove your mastery by *building* CC extensions, not by reading about them.

## Format

- **2-week sprints** for Sprints 1 & 2; **5-week capstone** for Sprint 3.
- **4-person teams**, reshuffled each sprint, with a no-repeat invariant — you will not be on a team with the same person twice.
- **Weekly async YouTube lectures** with live Mon/Wed in-class discussion.
- **Individual Claude Code artifacts** every 2–3 weeks.
- **AI-graded** sprint and artifact submissions; instructor reviews and may override.

## AI use policy

AI use is required, expected, and welcomed — Claude Pro is your textbook. **Cite all AI assistance at the top of any code you submit.** "Used Claude Sonnet 4.6 for the LLM-backend Strategy refactor" is appropriate; pretending you wrote what an LLM produced is plagiarism.

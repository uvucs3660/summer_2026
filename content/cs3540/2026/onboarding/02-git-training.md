## Why this matters more than you think

In most courses, you can survive a semester knowing `add`, `commit`, and `push`, because your work
lives in a repository only you touch.

Not here. The class engine spec is **one repository that the entire class writes to**. From Week 2
onward you will be branching, opening pull requests, reviewing someone else's prose, and resolving
merge conflicts against edits made by a classmate.

Pay particular attention to the branching, pull request, and merge conflict sections. Those are the
parts you will use in ten days.

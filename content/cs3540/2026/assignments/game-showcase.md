# The Showcase

**Due:** finals week, Dec 7–11 · **Points:** 50

## What happens

Your assigned final exam slot. Games run, people play them, you answer questions.

Fifteen minutes each:

1. **Play it** — five minutes of someone else driving. Not a walkthrough; hand over the controls.
2. **Show the seam you are proudest of** — the code, briefly.
3. **Say what broke** — and what you would do with another two weeks.
4. **Questions.**

## What to bring

A build that runs without your laptop and without the campus network. Have a local fallback for every provider — this is the night quotas run out, and the game that keeps playing while its NPC dialogue degrades to authored lines demonstrates the thing the course actually taught.

## Submit

The commit URL of your final tag, plus your trailer.

## Acceptance criteria

- The game runs and is played by someone who did not build it.
- You can explain any part of it you are asked about.
- Degradation is demonstrated, not just claimed.

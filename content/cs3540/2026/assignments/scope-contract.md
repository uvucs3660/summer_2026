# Scope Contract

**Due:** Sun Oct 25, 2026 23:59 MT · **Points:** 25

## What to do

Fill in `scope-contract.md` in your portfolio repo (the template is already there) .

Declare: solo or team · the game in one sentence · which deliverables you are committing to · your asset budget · what you will cut first if something does not land.

## How this is graded

Your game is graded on **completeness against what you declare here**, not against an absolute size. A solo student and a four-person team can both score full marks. A team of four declaring a solo-sized game fails on scope rather than on quality.

**Declaring less and delivering it beats declaring more and not.** Every unticked box is a scope decision, which is legitimate — but it has to be made now, not in December.

## Teams

Solo or team is your choice. Team members submit identical contracts naming the same members.

## How this is graded

**Push to your repository.** The autograder runs on the push and posts its
feedback as a **GitHub issue** on that repo, scored against the rubric below.
Read the issue; that is where your feedback lives.

There is nothing to submit in Canvas. Your commit history *is* the submission,
and the commit timestamp is what the late policy measures.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every deliverable box ticked or unticked deliberately.
- Multiplayer mode named, or explicitly declared out of scope.
- Asset budget stated per modality.
- At least one named risk with what you would cut.

# Playtest Evidence

**Due:** Sun Nov 22, 2026 23:59 MT · **Points:** 60

## What to do

Run at least three sessions with people who **did not build your game**, and commit the notes to `codex/playtests/`. Submit the commit URL.

## The protocol

**Say nothing.** No tutorial, no hints, no "oh, you have to press E." The instant you explain something you destroy the only chance you had to learn whether the game explains it. Sit on your hands.

**Write, do not talk.** Timestamp and behavior:

```
0:14  clicked the HUD twice — expected a button
0:31  walked past the door, came back, walked past again
1:05  died. said "what killed me?"
2:40  never picked up the second weapon
4:12  stopped playing
```

**Note every pause.** Hesitation means the game asked a question they could not answer.

**Ask afterwards, about intent.** "What were you trying to do there?" — not "did you like it?"

## What is graded

Behavior, not opinion. "They liked it" is unusable. And **at least one specific change you made because of an observation**, linked to that observation — a playtest that changed nothing was a demo.

## Acceptance criteria

- Three or more outside testers; teammates do not count.
- Notes are behavioral and timestamped, including where each one stopped.
- At least one change traced to the observation that prompted it.

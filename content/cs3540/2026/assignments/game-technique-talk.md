# Game Technique Talk

**Due:** materials by Thu Dec 3, 2026 · presented weeks 9–16 · **Points:** 100

## What to do

Fifteen minutes teaching the class the technique **your spec section** specifies. You present on your assigned day; commit slides or notes to `presentations/game-technique.md` .

## What makes it work

1. **A mental model first.** One idea, stated plainly, before any code.
2. **A worked example.** Something concrete they could attempt afterwards.
3. **The failure mode.** How it breaks, and what that looks like.
4. **Your ambiguity.** Where your section was underdetermined, what diverged, and how you fixed it.

Point 4 is not optional and not a confession. Every section had one — mine had two before a single student saw it. Claiming there was no divergence when the report says otherwise scores zero on that criterion.

## What does not work

Reading your section aloud. They can read. Teach them the thing the section is *about*.

- Fits fifteen minutes.
- Mental model, worked example, and failure mode all present.
- A real divergence discussed with its resolution.

**Recorded:** Sun Sep 6, 2026 · **Points:** 40

## How it works

Weeks 1–3. **Two unexcused absences across the whole term cost nothing.** Each additional absence removes 20% of the attendance component.

## Why it is graded at all

A student presentation opens nearly every session. Attendance here is something you owe your classmates an audience for, not something you owe me.

## Missing a session

Tell me beforehand where you can. Illness, work, and emergencies are not the thing this policy is aimed at — a habit of not coming is.

If you present on a day you cannot attend, arrange a swap with someone and tell me. Do not simply not appear; someone else prepared for that slot.

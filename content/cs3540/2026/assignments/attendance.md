# Attendance

**Recorded through the term · Points:** 100

Instructor-recorded. Nothing to submit, and nothing to push.

## How it works

**Two unexcused absences across the whole term cost nothing.** Each absence after that removes 20% of this component.

Recorded per session and entered once at the end of the term, so the grade you see here is final rather than moving week to week.

## Why it is graded at all

A student presentation opens nearly every session — *My Favorite Game* through Week 8, then *Game Technique* on the spec section its author owns. Those only work if there is a room to present to.

Attendance here is something you owe your classmates an audience for, not something you owe me.

## Missing a session

Tell me beforehand where you can. Illness, work, and emergencies are not what this policy is aimed at — a habit of not coming is.

If you are scheduled to present on a day you cannot attend, **arrange a swap with someone and tell me**. Do not simply not appear; another person prepared for that slot.

## Late arrival

Arriving after the presentation has finished means you missed the part that cannot be repeated. It is not recorded as an absence, but it is the thing this component exists to protect.

# Forge 08 — A Council Run

**Due:** Sun Nov 15, 2026 23:59 MT · **Points:** 60

## What to build

Three independent perspectives on one **real** decision from your project, one synthesized ruling, and the dissent preserved.

## Evidence — half the grade

The deliberation, the ruling, and the recorded dissent — all committed.

Commit both the artifact and its evidence to `forge/08-council/` in your portfolio repo:

```
forge/08-council/
  README.md      what it does, why you built it, what it must not do
  <the artifact>
  evidence/      transcripts and output
```

Submit the commit URL.

## Notes

The decision has to be genuinely open, with the perspectives disagreeing about something that mattered. Three subagents in one turn run in parallel. UVU's AI Gateway gives you four vendors under one login, which beats one model roleplaying three positions.

## Acceptance criteria

- The artifact exists and is demonstrated working in **your own** repository.
- It solves a problem you actually had — a generic version any project could use unchanged scores half.
- `evidence/` contains a record of it running, including output.

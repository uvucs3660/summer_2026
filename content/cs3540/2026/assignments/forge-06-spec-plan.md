# Forge 06 — A Spec and Plan

**Due:** Sun Nov 1, 2026 23:59 MT · **Points:** 60

## What to build

A specification and an implementation plan for one piece of your game, where each plan step is executable in a single session by an agent with no other context.

## Evidence — half the grade

Execute at least one step and record what happened — including if it went wrong.

Commit both the artifact and its evidence to `forge/06-spec-plan/` in your portfolio repo:

```
forge/06-spec-plan/
  README.md      what it does, why you built it, what it must not do
  <the artifact>
  evidence/      transcripts and output
```

Submit the commit URL.

## Notes

The test of a good step: could one agent, in one session, complete it and know it succeeded? If a step cannot be verified, split it until it can.

## Acceptance criteria

- The artifact exists and is demonstrated working in **your own** repository.
- It solves a problem you actually had — a generic version any project could use unchanged scores half.
- `evidence/` contains a record of it running, including output.

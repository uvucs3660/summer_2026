# My Favorite Game — Talk

**Due:** materials by Sun Oct 11, 2026 · presented weeks 2–8 · **Points:** 60

## What to do

Teach the class a game you love. You present on your assigned day; commit slides or notes to `presentations/favorite-game.md` .

Fifteen minutes, three parts:

**1 · Why it works.** In the vocabulary of design — core loop, difficulty curve, feedback, telegraphing, what the pattern was and how long it took to learn. Convince someone who dislikes the genre.

**2 · The technical how.** At least one system, explained concretely enough that the class could attempt it. How did the thing actually work?

**3 · What you are stealing.** One idea going into your own project this term.

## What does not work

Plot summary. Nostalgia. "It was amazing at the time" is a review, not a talk.

The strongest version connects the two halves: **name the constraint the developers faced, then show the design decision that came out of it.** Space Invaders' escalating difficulty is a CPU running out of headroom. Silent Hill's fog is a draw-distance limit. See `cheatsheet-history-of-games`.

## Play it again first

Memory reconstructs games as better and simpler than they were. Play the original, not a remaster — the original constraints are the subject.

## Acceptance criteria

- Analysis in design vocabulary, not appreciation.
- One technical system explained at the level of how it was built.
- Something specific you are taking into your own work.
- Fits the time and handles questions.
